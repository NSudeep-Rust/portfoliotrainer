01 · Profile & Mission

Battle-Tested Training for Real-World Security

Transforming students and technical teams from passive listeners into active security analysts, penetration testers, and threat responders.

I am Jeevan Bagade, a freelance Certified Trainer based in Hubballi, Karnataka with 3+ years of experience conducting intensive workshops for universities, polytechnics, and corporate training vendors.

My educational background includes a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) from MIT World Peace University (MIT-WPU), Pune and a BCA from JSS SMI College, Dharwad. As a Certified Trainer, I bridge the gap between academic theory and offensive/defensive tradecraft.

Having delivered multi-day hands-on cybersecurity workshops across Hubballi, Gadag-Betageri, Gangawati, and Dharwad, I know exactly where university students struggle: moving from theoretical textbook concepts to practical, real-world exploitation and defense.

My training modules feature isolated local virtual laboratories (Kali Linux, Burp Suite, Metasploit, Wireshark, Splunk, Docker) where students attack and secure simulated web apps, networks, and cloud infrastructure with zero risk to host infrastructure.
